== Why the Division into two Areas?

Markup languages like AsciiDoc rely on a clear separation of content and presentation. This is also the case in *{app-name}*. You write in the editor on the left, and the text is rendered as HTML or PDF in the preview on the right. This has several advantages:

* *Efficiency*: Adjust content quickly without manually reworking every formatting detail.
* *Consistency*: Design changes automatically and uniformly affects the entire document.
* *Flexibility*: The same content can easily be output in various formats (e.g., PDF, HTML).

== Excellent Markups

AsciiDoc uses so-called _markup_ that encloses your text to emphasize content. Format text, for instance, in *bold* or _italic_.

.Here are a few more markups:

[grid=all, frame=none, stripes=even]
|===
|Command  |Keyboard Shortcut | Markup | Result

|Bold | kbd:[⌘+B] | \*bold* | *bold*
|Italic | kbd:[⌘+I] | \_italic_ | _Italic_
|Highlighted | kbd:[⌘+#] | \#highlighted# | #highlighted#
|Underlined | kbd:[⌘+U] | [.underline]\#underlined# | [.underline]#underlined#
|===
